Three principles, executed faithfully

Founded on mutual self-help, the society exists to champion the financial independence and welfare of staff at FMC Kumo.

Save consistently

Contributions are deducted through hospital payroll, so saving takes no effort and builds interest-free member reserves.

  • Automatic salary checkoff
  • Zero monthly maintenance charges

Invest ethically

Pooled funds go into audited consumer staples, bulk commodity procurement and low-risk hospital supply ventures.

  • No speculative trading
  • Bulk purchases for staff
  • Annual surplus dividend

Grow collectively

In an emergency, borrow up to twice your savings at modest single-digit rates, without collateral headaches.

  • Medical and tuition relief
  • Welfare and bereavement pool
  • Peer-guarantor endorsement

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers on eligibility, loans, contributions and retirement.

Membership is open to all permanent and confirmed staff across every directorate of Federal Medical Centre, Kumo (Clinical, Nursing, Pharmacy, Admin, Laboratory, Works and others) after completing the enrollment form.
New members qualify for the standard 2× loan after six (6) consecutive months of salary checkoff. Emergency welfare facilities are available after three (3) months.
