Three Principles, Executed Faithfully

Founded on mutual self-help, the society exists solely to champion the financial autonomy and welfare of civil servants at FMC Kumo.

1. Save Consistently

Deducted seamlessly via hospital payroll checkoff, eliminating deposit friction while accumulating interest-free member reserves.

  • Automatic salary checkoff
  • Zero monthly maintenance charges

2. Invest Ethically

Pooled member resources are safely deployed into audited consumer staples, bulk commodity procurement, and low-risk hospital supply syndicates.

  • Zero speculative trading
  • Direct hospital equipment bulk purchases
  • Annual surplus dividend allocation

3. Grow Collectively

When emergencies arise, members borrow up to twice their cumulative balance at modest single-digit processing rates without collateral headaches.

  • Emergency medical & tuition relief
  • Welfare & bereavement support pool
  • Friendly peer-guarantor endorsement
